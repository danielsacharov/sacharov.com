  1. Start
  2. Results into action

Context

  • Germany ranks only 16th out of 17 countries in digital health.
  • Patient involvement often ends with receiving a PDF.
  • As a result, 75.8% struggle to understand and use digital health info.

Competitive analysis reveals an existing problem: Even among competitors, the way results are presented fails to convince users. English translation: "[...] I do not find the evaluation 100% understandable."

Problem

Most patients receive their medical results as static PDFs without explanation, making it difficult to interpret what the values actually mean.

The Idea

  • Digitalization: Native app instead of paper/PDF—health data where users are
  • Beyond PDF: Ongoing support: understand results, track changes, learn for everyday life
  • Digital competence: Plain language, visuals, guided flows—lower the barrier for the 75.8%
